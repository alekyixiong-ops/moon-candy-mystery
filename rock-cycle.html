🔄 THE ROCK CYCLE

Charlotte's Rock Detective Field Guide · how all rocks turn into each other

🔄 Earth's recycling machine

There is no such thing as a rock that lasts forever. Every rock on Earth is on a journey — and the journey goes round and round. Heat from deep inside the Earth and energy from the Sun keep the machine running. It has been running for 4.5 billion years.

Rock is not a thing. Rock is a stage. Your granite is a stage. Your gypsum is a stage. Give them millions of years and they'll both become something else. 🐢

🔧 The four machines that change rocks

ProcessWhat happensMakes
🌊 Weathering & erosionrain, wind, ice, waves, tree roots break rock into little bits (sediment)sand, mud, pebbles
🚚 Transport & settlingrivers and waves carry the bits away and drop them in layerslayers on a sea floor
💪 Compaction & cementinglayers get buried, squeezed, and glued together🏖️ sedimentary rock
♨️ Heat & pressureburied rock gets cooked and squeezed — but does NOT melt💎 metamorphic rock
🔥 Meltingit gets so hot it turns to liquid🌋 magma
❄️ Coolingmagma cools — fast = tiny crystals, slow = big crystals🔥 igneous rock

💡 The trick to remember: heat + squeeze = metamorphic. Full melt = igneous. Bits glued together = sedimentary.

🪨 The journey of YOUR granite

Let's follow your specimen around the loop. This is real — it just takes a very long time:

1️⃣ Now: granite, speckly with quartz, feldspar and mica. Sitting in a school bag. 😊
2️⃣ Weathering: rain and wind slowly crack it. The feldspar rots into clay, the mica washes away, the quartz crumbles into sand. (This is already happening to Hong Kong's granite!)
3️⃣ Travel: a river carries the sand to the sea and drops it in layers.
4️⃣ Burial: thousands of years of layers pile on top, squashing and gluing it → sandstone (sedimentary!). 🏖️
5️⃣ Deeper: it sinks and heats up. Cooked and squeezed but not melted → quartzite (metamorphic!). 💎
6️⃣ Even deeper: it gets so hot it melts → back to magma. 🌋
7️⃣ Cooling slowly deep underground → granite again! 🔥 And the cycle starts over.

So your little rock has probably been through this loop more than once in 4.5 billion years. 🐢

🇬🇯 Where each family lives in Hong Kong

  • 🔥 Igneous — everywhere! Lion Rock is granite; Sai Kung's hexagon columns are tuff. HK is a volcanic city. 🌋
  • 🏖️ Sedimentary — rare in HK (our rocks are young and were never sea floor for long) — that's why your gypsum was a lucky find! 🍀
  • 💎 Metamorphic — also rare here. Your best chance is marble floors, tiles and counters in buildings. 🏠

💡 In Hong Kong, igneous rocks are the local ones — the others usually arrived as building stone. So check buildings!

🤯 Mind-blowers from the rock cycle

  • 🍏 Marble is cooked limestone! Same stuff as chalk — just heated until the crystals grew. That's why marble sparkles and chalk doesn't.
  • 📋 Slate is squashed shale. Compress mud-rock hard enough and it splits into flat sheets — perfect roof tiles.
  • 💎 Diamond is metamorphic carbon! Squeeze carbon a hundred miles down and you get the hardest natural thing on Earth.
  • 🌌 Sand is the great recycler. There are grains of sand on beaches that have passed through the whole cycle many times over.
  • 🐢 Speed check: the cycle runs in millions of years. A human life is a blink of its eye.
