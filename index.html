CHARLOTTE'S MYSTERY · SEPTEMBER 11

Chapter 1 — The Vanished Sparkle

One morning, on the far side of Furry Friends Island, something terrible happened: Glimmer the Sparkle-Bunny woke up... and her sparkle was GONE! 💔

Her magic nose was still there. Her bunny-unicorn ears still sparkled like fireworks! But the little star-trail she was famous for? Not a single twinkle.

Poor Glimmer hopped across the Moon-Candy Forest and left only ordinary paw prints. She sat down by the Moonberry Pond and giggled — but even her jingle-bell laugh sounded flat. 🎵🔕

Chapter 2 — The Letter

That's when you got the letter. 📜

"Dear Detective Charlotte, Please help! My sparkle has vanished and only a true detective can find it. Come to Moon-Candy Forest — but beware, there are clues everywhere, and one of them is a ROTTEN TRICK. — Glimmer"

You grabbed your magnifying glass 🔍 and set off across the island. Your brother Chace wanted to come, but he was busy having a banana — his favorite, the one he calls "nana!" 🍌 So you went alone.

Chapter 3 — Clue #1: The Balloon

At the edge of the forest you found a strange, sagging thing half-buried in the leaves — a deflated party balloon, all wrinkled and sad. 🎈

You knew all about balloons that lose their gas — you'd invented the Forever Float balloon to stop that leak! But around its neck hung a label that read:

"I once floated tall and proud / But my secret leaked right out of me. / If you want Glimmer's stars, look close / At what's NOT where it should be."

Interesting. You tucked it in your pocket.

Chapter 4 — Clue #2: The Footprints

Deeper in, past the glittery Moonberry trees 🌙🫐, you found Clue #2: a row of tiny footprints leading to the pond... but they were going the WRONG way — into the water, not out!

And beside the pond lay a single, dry moonberry, rolled far away from its bush.

Who would leave a moonberry behind? You remembered that moonberries are Glimmer's favorite. Something was making her forget the things she loved.

Chapter 5 — Clue #3: The Note

At last you reached her cozy little hollow. On the doorstep sat Clue #3 — a note written in shaky handwriting:

"I didn't mean to, but I was so hungry and tired and THIRSTY. I just wanted to feel cool and floaty again. If I tell you the truth, will you still be my friend?"

You frowned, reading it again... and again. Three clues. A deflated balloon. Backwards footprints heading INTO the water. A forgotten moonberry. A note about being thirsty, about wanting to "feel cool and floaty."

Chapter 6 — The Sparkle Comes Back

And then — like a sparkle finally switching back on — you knew. 💡

You knelt by Glimmer's hollow and said softly: "Glimmer... you're not sad. You're DRY. Your sparkle didn't vanish — it just fizzled out because you haven't had a drink all day! The balloon popped from a little leak. Your footprints led into the pond because you went to splash in the water to cool off. You forgot your moonberry because you were too tired to remember. The sparkle is still in you, Glimmer — it's just thirsty!" 🫧

Glimmer's eyes went wide. She hopped to the pond and took the biggest, sparkliest sip of cool moon-water she'd ever had... and POOF! ✨ her little star-trail flickered back to life, brighter than before!

The End 🎁

"You solved it, Detective!" Glimmer giggled — and now her laugh rang like a hundred tiny bells. 🎵🔔

As the sun set over Furry Friends Island, Glimmer hopped home leaving a glittering trail of pink stars... all the way to your own doorstep. She'd saved the prettiest star for her favorite detective. 🌟
