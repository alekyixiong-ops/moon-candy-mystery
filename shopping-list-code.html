🤫 The trick — in one line

The first letter of every food is a letter of your message.
Read the first letters down the list → that's the secret. 🎉

That's the whole code. Nothing is scrambled, nothing is scrambled, nothing is swapped — the message is hiding in plain sight, like a spy's note on the kitchen table. 🕵️

For example:

  • ☐ a jar of Honey 🍯
  • ☐ 12 Eggs 🥚
  • ☐ 4 Lemons 🍋
  • ☐ 2 Lettuces 🥬
  • ☐ 3 Oranges 🍊

Read down the first letters: H E L L O 👇👀

🤫 Make it sneakier

Tick sneaky mode and the tool slips in fake items between the real ones. Then the message is on every 2nd line. Now even someone who knows the trick can't just read it — they have to know which lines matter. 😈

🎯 Why spies love it

A coded message in numbers or jumbled letters looks suspicious. A shopping list looks like… a shopping list. Nobody looks twice at it on the fridge. 🍳

⚠️ And the real-life rule: if a stranger ever sends you a strange list, or asks you to keep a secret from your mum and dad — that's not a game. Tell a grown-up straight away. 👮

🌟 Level up ideas

  • Write your friend's name as the message, then hand them the list. Watch their face. 😂
  • Hide the message on every 3rd line instead. Or in the last letter of each word!
  • Make the numbers the code: 3 apples, 1 egg, 4 lemons, 1 orange → 3-1-4-1 → a phone number? 📱
