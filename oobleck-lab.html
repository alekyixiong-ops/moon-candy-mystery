🟢 THE OOBLECK LAB

The liquid that turns into a solid · for Charlotte 🧙‍♀️

🥣 What is oobleck?

Oobleck is just cornflour (cornstarch) + water. That's it. Two boring things… that together become something impossible. 😱

It behaves like a liquid when you're gentle… and like a SOLID when you hit it. Same stuff. Different speed.

💡 Scientists call it a non-Newtonian fluid. Oobleck is shear-thickening — “shear” means pushing it sideways/fast, and it gets thicker when you do. 🧠

🧮 The recipe

1
🍞 cornflour1 cup
💧 water½ cup
🎨 food colouringa few drops
  • Tip the cornflour into a bowl.
  • Add the water a little at a time, stirring. You want it to feel like honey that won't pour. 🍯
  • If it's too runny → more cornflour. Too crumbly → a splash more water.
  • Add colour. Green is traditional (it's named after a Dr. Seuss story!) but any colour works. 🌈
  • Pick it up and squeeze! It goes hard. Open your hand and it drips away. 🤩
⚠️ Three oobleck rules
1. Never pour it down the sink — it hardens in pipes and blocks them! Put it in the bin. 🗑️
2. Do it on a tray or outside — it gets everywhere. 🍀
3. Ask a grown-up first. 🙋‍♀️

📋 The test list

  • Punch it → your fist bounces off. It's a solid! 💪
  • Press your finger in slowly → it sinks in like water. 💧
  • Scoop some up and squeeze → it makes a hard ball… then melts back into goo in your hand. ⚫
  • Try to stir it fast → the spoon gets stuck. Slow → it swirls happily.
  • Pour it from a height → it splats and cracks like a solid.
  • Drag a fork through it quickly → it tears into chunks.
  • Leave it alone for a minute → it slowly flattens out like a puddle. 🐢
🏆 The famous one: people have actually run across a swimming-pool full of oobleck! If you keep moving fast, it's a solid floor. If you stop… you sink straight in. 😱 (Ask a grown-up to look up the video!)

🧪 Why does it do that?!

Cornflour is made of millions of tiny grains, far too small to see. When you add water, the grains slide over each other easily — so it pours like a liquid. 💧

But when you push fast, the grains get jammed together. There's no time for them to slide out of the way! They lock into a solid wall. 🧱

⏱️ It's all about SPEED.
Slow → the grains have time to move = liquid.
Fast → the grains jam = solid.
Same oobleck. Same you. Just different speed! ⚡

🌍 Where it shows up in real life

  • Quicksand — the classic! Struggling fast makes it grip you harder. Move slowly and you float. 🏖️
  • Ketchup — shake it hard and it suddenly flows. (That one's the opposite way round!) 🌶️
  • Your blood — it gets thicker when it flows fast. 🩸
  • Body armour — engineers soak fabric in shear-thickening fluid. Normally soft — but it hardens instantly when a bullet hits. 🛡️

📖 The story it's named after

In 1949, Dr. Seuss wrote “Bartholomew and the Oobleck” — a king wishes for something new to fall from the sky, and the kingdom gets buried in sticky green goo. 🍀 A goo that turns hard when you hit it, and drips when you don't? It needed a name. So the gloop got the king's name. 😂
