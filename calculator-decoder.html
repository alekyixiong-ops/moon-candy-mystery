⚠️ THE #1 CALCULATOR TRAP: DEG vs RAD

Your Rad button switches the angle mode, and different modes give totally different answers!

  • DEG (degrees): a full circle = 360° → sin 30 = 0.5 ✅
  • RAD (radians): a full circle = 2π → sin 30 = −0.988 😱

Same buttons, wildly different answers — that's why everyone gets tricked at least once. If your trig answers look crazy, check the mode! 💡

🔑 THE SECRET SHIFT KEYS

2nd (or Shift / Alpha) is like the Shift key on a keyboard: press it first, and every button gets a second secret job.

  • 2nd + sin = sin⁻¹ (angle from a ratio)
  • 2nd + √ = ∛ (cube root)
  • 2nd + log = 10ˣ
  • 2nd + ln = eˣ

💡 If a button isn't doing what you expect, look for the tiny coloured writing above it — that's its 2nd job!
