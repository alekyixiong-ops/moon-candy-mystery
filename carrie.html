CARRIE

Guardian of the Land · Keeper of the Meadow
Inhabitant of Furry Friends Island

Who she is

SpeciesWhite Holland Lop 🐰
AppearancePure white, incredibly soft fur, fluffy cheeks and gentle blue eyes. She has a pair of elegant wings and perfectly trimmed, short whiskers.

The Soul-Jewel

ShapeCaria-shaped — the heart-petaled flower of the island 🌼
ColourVibrant green 💛
ConnectionHer jewel is a piece of the singular Mother-Crystal from the Caring Core. It acts as the battery for her unique magic — and for her ability to fly.

Unique Magical Abilities

While every inhabitant of the island has their own special power, Carrie's magic is deeply tied to the earth.

  • The Land-Speaker — Carrie can talk to and understand any creature that lives on the land. Whether it's a tiny beetle in the grass or the ancient roots of a Sky-Oak, she hears their stories and knows their needs.
  • Instant Growth (No Seeds Required) — Carrie is a living wand for nature. She doesn't need seeds to create life.
🐾 The Ritual: one paw on her green crystal, the other paw on the ground.
💬 The Command: she simply speaks the name of the plant — “Honey-Berry Bush!” — and it instantly sprouts and matures from the soil. 🌱
  • The Land-Bound Rule — her growth magic and communication are specialised for the land. While she can fly like everyone else, her power is the anchor that keeps the Meadow of Memories and Friendship Forest lush and thriving. 🌿

Personality & Role

🍏 The Provider

Because she can grow any plant at will, she makes sure there is always plenty of fresh Caria fruit and delicious snacks for her friends.

🤝 The Mediator

Since she can talk to all land creatures, she is often the one who helps solve misunderstandings between neighbours.

🧭 The Explorer

She loves flying to new parts of the island to see what kind of new plants the ground might want to grow there.
