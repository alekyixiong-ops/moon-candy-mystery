⭕ The circle makes sense of it all

Imagine a dot going round a circle of radius 1. Its height is sin, its width across is cos. That's why they're never bigger than 1! 😱

🎵 Now the magic: if you unroll that going-round movement into a line, you get a wave! That's the ribbon on the right. Sound, light, radio, tides, springs… all of them are made of sine waves. So sin and cos are secretly the shape of music. 🎶

🏔️ Measure a tree without climbing it

Real surveyors do this! Stand a known distance from the tree, look up to the top, and measure the angle. Then:

height = distance × tan(angle) 📈
–

💡 Sanity check: if the angle is 45°, tan = 1, so the tree is exactly as tall as your distance away! 🧠
