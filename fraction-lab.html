🍕 What IS a fraction?

A fraction is just a piece of a whole thing — like slices of a pizza. 🍕

The bottom number = how many equal pieces we cut the whole into. 🔪
The top number = how many pieces we take. 👈

📊 The same fraction as a bar

See? Same thing, drawn differently. A pie, a bar, a number — all the same fraction. 📈

⚖️ Which one is bigger?

Careful — a bigger bottom number makes the pieces SMALLER! 😱
(Would you rather have 1 slice of a pizza cut into 4, or 1 slice of a pizza cut into 12?)

12 ? 13
Pick one! 👇
💡 Trick: the same top number means the smaller bottom wins. 1/2 > 1/3 > 1/4 > 1/5 — the more pieces you cut, the tinier each one is! 🍕

➕ Adding and taking away

Same bottom number? Easy — just add the tops. 👍

Different bottoms? You must make them match first — like cutting both pizzas into the same size slices. 🔪

/ /
–
💡 The magic move: multiply the two bottom numbers to get a common denominator. Then both fractions have the same slice size and you can just add the tops! ✨
