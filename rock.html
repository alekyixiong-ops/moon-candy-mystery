🔍 Why that proves it's granite

You spotted all three minerals — that's the real scientist's test. Not many rocks show three different crystals you can see with your own eyes. Granite is one of the few. Congratulations, Detective — you identified a rock in the field. 🎉

Feldsparwhite / pink blocks. The most common mineral in granite. Weathers into clay.
Quartzglassy grey bits. Hardness 7 — harder than steel!
Micablack shiny flakes that catch the light. Softest of the three — it peels into sheets!

🔥 Its life story

Your rock began deep underground as molten magma — liquid fire. Then it cooled very slowly, over thousands of years, and that slow cooling let the crystals grow big enough for you to see. That's why granite is speckly and lava rocks are smooth: magma took its time, lava had to hurry. 🐢

🇭🇰 Bonus: your rock knows Hong Kong

Hong Kong sits on a huge granite body — Lion Rock 🦁 is granite! When HK's granite weathers, its feldspar rots into soft clay, which is why heavy rain can cause landslides here. Your little school rock is a piece of the city's bones. 🏗️
