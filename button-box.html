📈 log & ln — the “how many zeros?” buttons

log asks: “10 to the power WHAT gives this number?”

log 1000 = 3  →  because 10³ = 1000
💡 log is the opposite of 10ˣ. They undo each other!
log 100 = 2 · log 1000 = 3 · log 1,000,000 = 6 · log 1 = 0 🎯

ln is the same idea, but it uses the number e (2.718...) instead of 10. Scientists use ln for growth and radioactivity.

🎉 x! — the factorial button

x! means: multiply every whole number down to 1. It counts ways to arrange things! 🎲

5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120
🃏 How many ways to shuffle a deck of cards?
52! — that number is so huge that if every star in the universe shuffled cards every second since the Big Bang, they still wouldn't have seen them all. Not even close. 😱

Watch it explode: 10! = 3,628,800. By 20! you're in the quintillions (that's 19 digits!) — and your calculator gives up around 69! 💥

🌾 The rice-and-chessboard legend:
A king offered a reward: 1 grain of rice on square 1, 2 on square 2, 4 on square 3, doubling each time. By square 64 the pile would be 18,446,744,073,709,551,615 grains — more rice than the whole world grows in centuries! That's 2ⁿ for you. 😄

🔄 1/x — the flip button

Press 1/x and the number does a cartwheel: it becomes 1 ÷ the number. 🧠

1 ÷ 4 = 0.25
🎯 The pattern to spot:
Flip a big number → get a tiny one. Flip a tiny one → get a big one.
1 ÷ 1000 = 0.001  ↔  1 ÷ 0.001 = 1000 😱

⚠️ And 1 ÷ 0? That would need a number that × 0 gives 1 — impossible! That's why your calculator screams ERROR or Math ERROR. 🚫

🌱 eˣ & 10ˣ — the growth buttons

These are just powers of special numbers: 10ˣ is powers of ten, eˣ is powers of e = 2.718281828...

🌱 Why does a weird number like 2.718... get its own button?
Because it's the number of natural growth — things that grow in proportion to how big they already are: bacteria, money in the bank, radioactivity, rabbits. Mathematicians call it Euler's number. It even hides inside snail shells and sunflower seeds! 🌼

10⁵ = 10 · 10² = 100 · 10³ = 1000 ... but also 10⁻² = 0.01 ⬇

🎭 2nd — the secret Shift key

Everything on a scientific calculator has a hidden second job. Press 2nd and the whole keyboard flips over! Tap the buttons to see both sides 👇

Press 2nd, then tap a button above. 🎭
